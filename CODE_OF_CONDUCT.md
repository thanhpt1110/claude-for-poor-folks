# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise the code, not the person.

## The longer version

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

Examples of behaviour that makes this a good place to be:

- Giving feedback on what the code does, with a reproduction rather than an adjective.
- Accepting that a maintainer may say no, and that "no" is usually about scope.
- Saying when you were wrong. This project's own changelog does it repeatedly.

Examples of behaviour that is not welcome:

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report anything that needs attention to **thanhphantuan1110@gmail.com**. Reports are handled
privately. A maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, and may block accounts, for
behaviour they judge inappropriate.
